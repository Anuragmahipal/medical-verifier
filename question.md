2. Treatment options for endometriosis include both medical and surgical approaches. Medical treatments aim to manage symptoms and slow the progression of the disease.
3. GnRH analogues are a class of drugs that reduce estrogen production by downregulating the pituitary gland, which can help alleviate symptoms of endometriosis by inducing a hypoestrogenic state.
